# Reuse fixed garden tree shadows

The courtyard's 36 trunk/branch parts and 12 pine needle/twig parts are ordinary, static meshes with camera-selected near/far geometry. They previously redrew their complete sun shadows on every sunlight update, even though the building shadow cache was already available.

The app now includes their 48 LOD roots in that existing cache. Both children remain tracked; actual child visibility, geometry and world transforms are part of the cache signature. A near/far switch rebuilds before reuse. Animated broadleaf canopy cards and distant woodland leaves remain dynamic, and the independent sanctuary lamp still receives complete caster lists. No model simplification, texture change, animation change or new shadow storage was introduced. The existing cache remains about 128 MiB at desktop resolution, 32 MiB at mobile resolution.

`garden-shadow-cache-fixture.html` compares the previous building-only cache with the extended cache using actual temple, tree, pine, pond, forest, statue and interior assets. Six views at two wind times cover near, far and mixed LOD states. Each mode first rebuilds and then measures a warm render at the same view and time. All twelve final color images, 4096² sun shadows and 2048² sanctuary shadows are byte-identical. Warm sunlight updates remove 578,254–1,242,660 triangle submissions and 48 draw calls. Cold updates draw the same total geometry as before; extra signature checks and cache rebuilds at LOD changes still cost CPU work. These draw-count results do not establish a whole-scene FPS gain.

Evidence is saved in `evidence/garden-shadow-cache-browser.json`. Browser warning/error logs were empty. A focused test verifies LOD hysteresis, rebuild-on-change, warm reuse, and continuing dynamic-caster submission. All 225 automated tests pass. The broader commercial AAA fidelity and frame-rate requirements remain open.

The integrated runtime `53130baaa274` reports 162 tracked static meshes. Moving from the overview to the bridge increased builds from two to three while subsequent reuse increased from 355 to 710. The bridge screenshot retained water, fish, foliage and shadows without warnings/errors. The observed frame time was still 115.9 ms; this is not a controlled before/after benchmark and does not demonstrate a speed improvement. See `evidence/garden-shadow-cache-main.json` and `.png`.
