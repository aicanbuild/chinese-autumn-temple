# Per-tree visibility inside static forest groves

The 620 forest trees retain their original transforms, geometry, opacity, 40 m spatial grouping and shadow membership. Browsers supporting `WEBGL_multi_draw` now use BatchedMesh inside the same 292 groves, allowing native per-instance visibility checks for the main camera, water reflection and shadow cameras. Other browsers retain InstancedMesh. No asset or Blender model changed.

All groves of a prototype share one immutable public BufferGeometry, so the forest still uploads 12 prototype buffers rather than 292 duplicates. Each batch has an identical geometry slot at offset zero and independent instance textures/draw lists. This assumes static geometry and a shared scene lifetime: editing geometry slots or disposing one grove while siblings use its geometry is unsupported. There is additional per-grove instance-texture and culling overhead; this is not a memory or CPU improvement claim.

Leaf color, depth and distance shaders use the same wind deformation and inverse-transpose normal correction for both batching and instancing. Per-instance bounds include maximum wind displacement. Water half-space classification reads transformed batch bounds and excludes batches from static triangle partitioning. Batched forest remains excluded from the contact-AO pass, matching the previous instanced behavior. Existing water capture cropping and spatial grouping remain in place.

`forest-culling-fixture.html` renders the actual temple, pond, stone, bridge, lantern and forest assets at six cameras and two wind times (0 and 13.7 s), with 4096-pixel sun shadows. The comparison includes the existing old-path vegetation shadow-prefix optimization. All 12 comparisons reduce triangle submissions by 490470–765016 (5.6–11.5% of these fixture frames) and draw calls by 12–34. Nine final-color images are byte-identical; the other three differ in 4, 1 and 3 channels respectively out of 3276800. One isolated channel differs by 43; other differences are one level. This is not an exact-color equivalence claim. Packed shadow bytes differ due to arithmetic/order precision; maximum decoded depth difference is 0.000003756, below 1.1% of the existing shadow bias, with zero pixels exceeding that bias.

Evidence: `evidence/forest-culling-browser.json`, fixed before/after screenshots and the full-scene screenshot/console report. The full test suite passes 205 tests, including original transforms, shared buffers, conservative wind bounds and batched water classification. No whole-scene FPS improvement has been established. The fixture omits some full-scene detail and postprocessing; commercial AAA quality and performance remain unfinished.
