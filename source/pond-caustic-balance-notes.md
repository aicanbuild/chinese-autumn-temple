# Calmer pool-bottom caustic contrast

The reference's pond reads as water and reflected surroundings before its small surface details. Close inspection of the temple pond showed bright caustic lines competing with the sediment texture and koi markings. The mean-one photon-density modulation now blends at 0.48 rather than 0.80 before the existing depth attenuation. That reduces the linear light-pattern deviation from its local mean by 40%; it is an art-direction adjustment, not a new physical scattering simulation or a claim that pixel brightness drops by 40%.

The density maps, animation phase, depth interpolation, shadow lookup, refracted sunlight direction, absorption and reflection/refraction captures remain unchanged. A shared contrast uniform lets the source comparison fixture show both values without reloading or changing the sun/camera. The production interface has no additional control. No model, image asset, extra map sample or render pass was added.

`pond-caustic-balance-fixture.html` uses the real temple, bridge, pond stones, koi and water at a frozen time. Both variants have the same shadowed sunlight and water setup. The source fixture omits newer full-scene plants and postprocessing. Evidence images `pond-caustic-balance-before.png` and `pond-caustic-balance-after.png` show the same close camera; a second camera verified bridge shadows. Calls and triangles match across the original/selected pair (47 calls and 849,817 triangles in the renderer's recorded final pass). Console warnings/errors are empty. Nine existing water/pond lighting checks passed. This is visual tuning, not a frame-rate improvement. Overall AAA fidelity remains incomplete.
