# Pond-bank moss and ground mapping

Twenty small irregular moss patches follow the actual bank-stone and terrain surfaces. `build_bank_moss.py` raycasts a world-space BVH built from the existing native objects, buries roots 1.5 mm, and aligns each shoot to its support normal. The 7,237 shoots have curved ridged leaves, varied green vertex colors and matte lighting. Existing meshes are preserved. Editable native outputs are `池岸立体苔藓.blend` and `听秋寺院.blend`; these remain local.

The exported Draco asset has 405,272 triangles in four spatial batches, with 18-bit position quantization to retain the tiny leaves. Decoded geometry has zero degenerate triangles. Static 1.5 m cells omit offscreen triangles only for the main camera, retaining the original geometry for shadow/other camera passes. The close-view fixture compares full and culled framebuffers at three viewpoints: zero changed channels in every view; submitted triangles respectively fell from 829,792 to 688,056, 829,792 to 664,704, and 932,664 to 664,704. These are fixture measurements, not whole-app frame-rate results.

Close inspection also exposed long stripes in the earlier garden-ground shader. The stripes persisted without shadows. Measuring 5,228 nearby terrain vertices found compressed UV-to-position errors of up to 0.09550 m (mean 0.04957 m). The moss color, roughness and normal now use interpolated world coordinates and a matching derivative tangent frame, eliminating this compressed-UV dependency. Original terrain geometry and the distant woodland material remain unchanged.

Validation: 10 ground/leaf/pond-stone tests and four static-cell tests passed. The browser fixture and actual courtyard rendered without warnings/errors. Evidence is in `evidence/bank-moss-browser.json`, `bank-moss-near.png`, `bank-moss-mapping-before.png`, and `bank-moss-main.png`. The fixture intentionally omits water and surrounding architecture; the main screenshot verifies integration. Fine moss adds geometry and is most visible close to the bank; this does not complete the overall AAA fidelity/performance goal.
