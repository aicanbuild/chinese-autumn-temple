# Gentle motion for the two pond lotus patches

Five existing material meshes (136,965 vertices) now share a continuous world-space bending field. It gradually grows from zero below −1.05 m, keeping the buried roots fixed. The authored geometry, flowers, leaf veins, UVs and materials remain intact. Leaf height never changes, avoiding water-surface bobbing or dipping. The sum of three slow noncommensurate phases moves the visible leaves and flowers by less than 31 mm. This is restrained procedural wind, not a fluid/stem simulation.

The same time and deformation functions drive the color, directional-depth, point-distance and SSAO-normal shaders. Normals use the exact inverse-transpose deformation Jacobian. The normal override supplies explicit zero generic attributes for unrelated geometry, so the rest of the temple stays static. Root geometry and all GLBs/Blender files are unchanged. The added per-vertex weight/slope attribute uses approximately 1.05 MiB.

Conservative motion padding is added to visibility bounds. Shader-deformed lotus is no longer split into fixed above/below-water indices, and its custom shadow materials exclude it from the static sunlight cache. Water captures therefore draw the same pose as the main camera. Baked sediment contact remains the authored static approximation: buried roots are fixed and movement near the bed is very small, but that atlas is not a dynamic contact solve.

AO refresh is limited to visible bounded animation. A known 0.021 m/s speed bound determines its refresh interval from conservative view-space bounds and projected motion, targeting about 0.35 pixel of movement, clamped to 16–400 ms. Unknown/skinned animation keeps the prior 80 ms fallback. Camera/geometry/settings changes still refresh immediately. This avoids constant full-scene AO refresh for pond plants behind the indoor camera. Changing plant geometry/animation must update its declared motion envelope and speed.

Validation: all 240 tests pass, including fixed roots/height, bounded displacement, independent finite-difference Jacobian checks, shared color/shadow/AO phase, conservative water visibility, static sunlight exclusion and distant/close/offscreen AO behavior. The actual Blender asset was rendered against CPU-deformed geometry at 0, 4 and 9 seconds. Color differences including sun and point shadows affect 1–5 channels of 1,680,000, at most one 8-bit level. Normal-pass differences affect 28–38 channels, with a maximum of 8 and mean below 0.000028; this is not bit-exact normal equivalence. Motion changes 167,269 channels between phases 0 and 9. The first fixture run caught a shader preprocessor newline error, which was fixed before final full-scene validation.

Evidence: `evidence/pond-lotus-motion-browser.json`, `evidence/pond-lotus-motion-main.png`, and the final main-page state. The local runtime is d2b0a0fd6fd3. A close pond sample measured 52.8 ms/frame and 15.2 ms CPU; this differs from the earlier camera and is not a controlled FPS comparison. Added dynamic shadows and normal refresh have a cost. Overall commercial AAA fidelity and sustained performance remain incomplete.

Integrated indoor check: with the pond out of view, AO captures stayed at 2,518 while cached reuses increased from 3,422 to 5,582. Warning/error logs were empty. Returning to the courtyard succeeded.
