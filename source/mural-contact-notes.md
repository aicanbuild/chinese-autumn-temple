# Mineral murals seated within their wall frames

The four wall paintings retain their existing artwork, proportions, plaster normal maps and roughness. A separate finite-distance ambient-visibility atlas adds the missing contact beneath the actual gilt/wood mouldings. It is baked from the furnished Blender temple, with inward-facing wall receivers 0.2 mm in front of the painted planes and a 16 cm ray limit. The side-wall receiver normals are explicit; the original paint meshes are double-sided.

`bake_mural_contact.py` reads but does not modify `听秋寺院.blend`, keeps only nearby faces, and packs the result in the independent editable `source/壁画嵌框接触光照.blend`. Native preview foliage is excluded because the browser replaces it with moving leaves. Four padded atlas regions avoid inter-panel filtering; artwork UV0 and metre-scale plaster UV1 remain unchanged, with contact on UV2.

Inspecting the atlas exposed two retained legacy gilt fasteners protruding through the rear paintings at native coordinates `(±2.8, 10.13, 3.75)`. The initial suspicion of foliage was disproven: removing preview leaves left identical pixels, and independent normal rays identified `Satin gilded bronze`. `inspect_mural_intrusions.py` measured two separate 65-vertex, 64-face components, about 3.8 cm across. `remove_mural_intrusions.py` backs up the complete temple, removes only those 130 vertices/128 faces, then reopens the saved file and verifies all other face positions and transforms across 7949 objects are unchanged. The same narrow regions are added to the existing browser replacement layout and the full construction script. Neither original painting nor the standalone sanctuary geometry is repainted or re-exported. The final atlas is rebaked from the corrected full native scene; it does not simply paint over the artifacts.

`installMuralContact` validates every mural vertex against its authored wall plane before changing any material or UVs. Only two existing materials receive the non-color 2048×1024 atlas, at intensity .85. No geometry, drawing batches, lights or reflection captures are added. The map affects indirect illumination through Three's standard AO path; it does not replace direct-light shadows or simulate full global illumination. Changes to the wall/frame layout require a fresh audit or bake, with current geometry/texture hashes recorded in `mural-contact-ao.json`.

Verification evidence is retained under `evidence/mural-contact-*`. The original broad AAA fidelity and performance objective is still incomplete.

Final verification: 102 independent ray probes across four panels, mean absolute error 0.001221 and maximum 0.004990. Five actual browser views preserve direct-only shading and shadow maps; the AO change only darkens ambient contact and adds no draw calls. Both removed fastener positions are clear in native geometry and runtime ray tests. The combined interior update retains this wall-contact asset unchanged.
